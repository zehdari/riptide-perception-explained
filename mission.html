Mapping · Chapter 10

One mission in Nereus

Every earlier chapter in one run: Talos's whole software stack swimming the RoboSub 2026 course in the simulator.

The short version
  • The full stack ran the competition tree in Nereus twice: on the mapped course, and with the course moved 72 cm (the torpedo another 1.27 m).
  • On the exact course every prop is confirmed, the table is fit and every task runs.
  • On the moved course the slalom puts the whole map in the right place; the badly placed torpedo is partly explained as odometry drift instead.

The run

Nereus simulates the pool, props, Talos's dynamics and sensors, and renders both ZED cameras' colour and depth. Everything else is the robot's own software, unchanged: YOLO and tensor_detector, mapping, the EKF, the MPC and RepairCompMpcTree. Nothing in the stack knows where the simulator put the props.

The map is init_data, the RoboSub 2026 layout as the team measured it. Two runs:

  • Exact course. Every prop exactly where the map says.
  • Moved course. Every prop shifted 0.6 m east and 0.4 m south (72 cm) (the offset mapping estimates), and the torpedo another 0.9 m east and 0.9 m north (a 1.27 m layout deviation).

Both ended SUCCESS. The false slalom pole on a gate leg (Chapter 3) is harmless: the map is locked through the gate.

Every prop against the truth

On the moved course every prior starts 72 cm off (the torpedo 1.58 m). On the exact course errors stay at a few centimetres: the slalom ends 3 cm off, the torpedo 9 cm.

What mapping said

Mapping logs only when something changes. Moved-course lines, with the tree's target changes; tap one to move the replay there.

What the simulator does and doesn't show

  • Depth is clean. Detections scatter about ten times less than the noise model assumes (Chapter 6). Real stereo depth is noisier.
  • Odometry is good. The EKF drifted under 15 cm over the 133 m run. On the robot the offset must absorb real drift.
  • The start-time drift is huge. In simulation the node reads the clock before the first /clock message, so its first predict adds about 1.8 × 109 s of drift to the offset's prior (17 900 m² at the start). On the robot it starts at the configured 1 m. The runs here are barely affected, but it is worth fixing in mapping.py: set last_predict_time on the first odometry or detection, not in the constructor.
  • Everything else is real: models, thresholds, filters, the tree's checks and timing.

Data comes from ros2 bag recordings via tools/bag_to_mission.py.
