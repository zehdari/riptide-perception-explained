Vision · Chapter 5

Props made of several pieces

The network sees symbols, not props. A torpedo board is four symbols and four identical rings; a gate half is two signs. These objects can only be built once the whole frame has been seen.

The short version
  • Gate signs: two symbols seen together become one gate_repair or gate_rescue.
  • Torpedo board: one plane through all its symbols; the four openings are named by sorting along the fire→blood axis, at any roll.
  • Slalom: a pole is too thin for a plane, so its position is one depth sample; only the nearest pole is reported.
  • The table pair exists in code but is off; mapping fits the table to the baskets.

Three groups skip Chapter 4's one-box-one-detection path and are resolved after the whole frame is routed (process). They publish names the network never outputs (gate_repair, torpedo, fire_hole_large), and those are what mapping tracks.

Pairs

Each 2026 gate half has a sign with two symbols. Autonomy needs the half, so the detector merges them (DETECTION_PAIRS):

  • compass + hammer_and_wrench → gate_repair
  • buoy + sos → gate_rescue
  • warning + helmet → table, only after set_table_pair_enabled(true); off in the current tree

With both members in the frame, each box is sampled as in Chapter 4, the points are pooled and one plane is fitted. More points over a wider span give a steadier normal. The position is the centre of the union of the boxes at the pooled mean depth; the confidence is the lower member's.

A lone member goes down the generic path under its own name (sos, compass), which mapping also knows. In the Nereus gate frames the network found sos and compass in separate frames, each just above 0.65, so no pair formed.

The table pair: switched off

warning + helmet (the table's two baskets) can merge into one flat table detection. RepairCompMpcTree turns it off at the table; mapping fits the table to many detections of each basket instead (mapping/center_table_on_baskets, Chapter 9).

The torpedo board

The board has four symbols (fire, blood, ambulance, fire engine) and four openings: a large and a small one near each of fire and blood. The network sees each opening only as circle, so geometry names them (process_torpedo_task). This runs only on the forward camera (torpedo_task_camera).

One plane for the whole board. Fire and blood are required, or nothing is published. Every visible symbol is sampled as in Chapter 4 and pooled into one SVD plane through the mean of the fire and blood centroids. Pooling matters: a fire symbol cut off by the image edge fit almost 50° off on its own.

Every centre on the plane. Each symbol and circle box centre is placed where its camera ray meets the plane:

\mathbf d = \frac{K^{-1}(u, v, 1)^\top}{\lVert K^{-1}(u, v, 1)^\top \rVert}, \qquad t = \frac{\bn \cdot \mathbf a}{\bn \cdot \mathbf d}, \qquad \mathbf p = t\,\mathbf d

\mathbf a: anchor point on the plane, \bn: board normal. An opening's middle is open water, so this is the only way to place it.

The board centre is the middle of the projected centres' extent in in-plane coordinates (u_k, v_k): \big(\tfrac{\min u_k + \max u_k}{2}, \tfrac{\min v_k + \max v_k}{2}\big). With only part of the board seen it is biased: in the first mission frame (about 2 m out) only fire cleared the threshold, and the detection landed 30 cm from the true centre. Confidence is the lower of fire's and blood's; orientation is the board normal.

Naming the openings needs fire, blood and exactly four circles. Project each opening onto the fire→blood axis, s_k = (\mathbf p_k - \mathbf p_{\text{fire}}) \cdot \hat{\mathbf s}, and sort: the two smallest are fire's, the two largest blood's. In each pair, the one nearer its symbol in 3D is large. Because it uses 3D distances on the board, it works at any roll and viewpoint; image-space rules break under perspective.

The slalom

A pole is a thin pipe with no surface to fit, so the slalom path is simple (_process_slalom):

  • Position: one depth sample at the box centre, back-projected. NaN or zero depth drops the pole.
  • Only the closest pole is kept (slalom_history_size = 1).
  • Orientation is not measured: it comes from slalom_parent_frame in TF (turned −90° about y), and mapping locks it to config anyway (lock_orientation_to_config). Only position counts.
  • The name comes from set_slalom_type (default slalom_front). The mission sets slalom_parent, so the closest pole updates the whole row.
